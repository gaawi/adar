<h1 class="adar-archive-title">Equilibrium</h1>
<p class="adar-archive-lede">
6th edition · Residency showcase. The trio The Rest Project opens up the work of its artistic residency at the second winning hórreo of the 2026 competition: La Casona de Belmonte.
</p>
</div>
</div>

<div class="adar-archive-body">
<p>
“Equilibrium” takes 3 as the number of balance —spirit, soul and body—: three musicians and three composers in harmony. Coinciding with the centenary of György Kurtág, Maria Florea (violin), Lara Fernández (viola) and Daniel Claret (cello) shared an open, still-in-progress programme, with the poetry of Joan Margarit resounding throughout the concert.
</p>
