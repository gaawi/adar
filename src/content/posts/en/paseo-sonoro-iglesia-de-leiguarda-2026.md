<h1 class="adar-archive-title">Sound walk in the Church of Leiguarda</h1>
<p class="adar-archive-lede">
6th edition. A sound walk inside the church of Leiguarda: the musicians spread across the nave and the audience moving among them, from one piece to the next.
</p>
</div>
</div>

<div class="adar-archive-body">
<p>
Natania Hoffman (cello), Frederieke Saeijs (violin), Guillermo Laporta (flute), Žilvinas Brazauskas (clarinet) and Josefina Urraca (piano) moved through solos, a duo and a closing trio —Bach, Ysaÿe, Rameau, Germanavičius and Mendelssohn—, with video art and light design by Guillermo Laporta. Žilvinas Brazauskas added the birbynė, Lithuania's traditional reed instrument, to the clarinet.
</p>
