<h1 class="adar-archive-title">Umbral Zero</h1>
<p class="adar-archive-lede">
At the Aula del Oro in Belmonte de Miranda —a former exhibition space of the municipality— Guillermo Laporta and Josefina Urraca offered an intimate night of minimalist music and visual art: music that tends towards zero, reduced to the smallest gesture until it grazes silence.
</p>
</div>
</div>

<div class="adar-archive-body">
<p>
A flute-and-piano programme that linked the minimalism of Max Richter with the bareness of Satie and Bach, alongside a recent piece by Zygmund de Somogyi for amplified flute and electronics. The visual projection, by Guillermo Laporta, was woven into the programme; the video art for the Bach Suite is by Guillermo Ordás, with Natania Hoffman on cello.
</p>

<h3 style="margin-top:28px">Programme</h3>
<p style="margin-top:18px">
<strong>Max Richter</strong> — Vladimir's Blues<br>
<strong>Lili Boulanger</strong> — Nocturne<br>
<strong>Erik Satie</strong> — Gymnopédie No. 1 (Lent et douloureux)<br>
<strong>Zygmund de Somogyi</strong> — imsotired (: (2023), for amplified flute and electronics<br>
<strong>Erik Satie</strong> — Gnossienne No. 3<br>
<strong>Johann S. Bach</strong> — Cello Suite No. 1 in G major, BWV 1007 (video: Natania Hoffman, cello; video art by Guillermo Ordás)<br>
<strong>Erik Satie</strong> — Gnossienne No. 1<br>
<strong>Aleksandr Scriabin</strong> — Romance
</p>
