<h1 class="adar-archive-title">The Shape of Memory</h1>
<p class="adar-archive-lede">
In the library of the Parador de Corias —a former 11th-century Benedictine monastery in Cangas del Narcea— the trio of María Martínez, Frederieke Saeijs and Josefina Urraca explored the ideas of remembrance and mourning with a chamber programme bringing together Classicism, Impressionism and recent creation.
</p>
</div>
</div>

<div class="adar-archive-body">
<p>
An intimate evening in one of the most secluded settings in western Asturias, linking the Classicism of Haydn, Lili Boulanger's Nocturne, Messiaen's Louange à l'immortalité de Jésus, Rachmaninoff's Trio élégiaque and Shostakovich's First Piano Trio, alongside a recent work by Brittney Benton.
</p>

<h3 style="margin-top:28px">Programme</h3>
<p style="margin-top:18px">
<strong>Lili Boulanger</strong> — Nocturne for violin and piano<br>
<strong>Brittney Benton</strong> — Piano Trio No. 1<br>
<strong>Sergei Rachmaninoff</strong> — Trio élégiaque No. 1 in G minor<br>
<strong>Joseph Haydn</strong> — Piano Trio<br>
<strong>Olivier Messiaen</strong> — Louange à l'immortalité de Jésus, from the Quartet for the End of Time<br>
<strong>Dmitri Shostakovich</strong> — Piano Trio No. 1, Op. 8
</p>
