<h2 class="amp-title">Artistic <em>proposals</em></h2>
<p class="amp-subtitle">Festival ADAR welcomes proposals from musicians, ensembles and artists interested in taking part in our programme in Asturias.</p>
</div>
</div>

<section class="ed-section rc-top">
<div class="ed-wrap">

<p class="ed-prose">The festival presents classical and chamber music, early music, contemporary composition, jazz, traditional music and interdisciplinary projects. Concerts take place in churches, heritage sites and rural settings, with a programme adapted to the characteristics of each venue.</p>

<p class="ed-prose">We particularly value proposals with a clear artistic identity. We are also interested in projects that include new work, educational activities, residencies or collaborations with the territory, although these are not requirements for taking part.</p>

</div>
</section>

<section class="ed-section" id="how-to-send">
<div class="ed-wrap">
<div class="ed-head">
<h2>How to send a proposal</h2>
</div>

<p class="ed-prose">You can write to us at any time of year at <a href="mailto:leiguardamusic@gmail.com?subject=PROPUESTA%20ADAR%20%C2%B7%20Name%20of%20the%20artist%20or%20ensemble">leiguardamusic@gmail.com</a>, with the subject line <strong>PROPUESTA ADAR · Name of the artist or ensemble</strong>. Please keep the first two words in Spanish, so that every proposal reaches the same folder.</p>


<p class="ed-prose">Please include:</p>

<ul class="rc-when-list rc-managers">
<li>Name of the artist or ensemble and a short biography.</li>
<li>Proposed programme, repertoire and approximate duration.</li>
<li>Links to recordings, preferably live.</li>
<li>Indicative fee, stating whether it includes travel and accommodation.</li>
<li>Basic technical requirements.</li>
<li>Availability during August 2027.</li>
</ul>

<p class="ed-prose">If you represent several artists, you are welcome to send us a selection of the proposals you consider most suitable for the festival.</p>

<p class="ed-prose">We review the proposals we receive while each edition is being prepared, and we get in touch when there is a possibility of working together.</p>

<p class="ed-prose">For co-production proposals, exchanges between festivals or international collaborations, you can write to us at the same address.</p>

<p class="rc-firma">Festival ADAR<br /><span>Leiguarda · Belmonte de Miranda · Asturias</span></p>

</div>
