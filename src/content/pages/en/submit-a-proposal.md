faq:
  - q: "What kind of music does Festival ADAR programme?"
    a: "Classical and chamber music, early music, jazz, experimental and electronic music, traditional music and music from other cultures, contemporary composition, and interdisciplinary projects combining sound with visual art, dance or video. We do not programme commercial music: rock, pop and related genres do not fit what we do or the spaces we play in."
  - q: "How do I send a proposal to Festival ADAR?"
    a: "By email to leiguardamusic@gmail.com, with the subject line «PROPUESTA ADAR · [name of the group or soloist] · [line-up]». There is no form. In the body of the message: who you are, links to recordings, the programme you propose, a short biography, technical requirements, your availability in August, a direct contact and your fee."
  - q: "Do I have to state my fee in the proposal?"
    a: "Yes, always. Without a fee we cannot assess the proposal. Give us the all-in fee per concert, whether it includes VAT, whether it covers travel, accommodation and per diems, and for how many people. Tell us as well if you have any mobility funding from your country or institution."
  - q: "Do you reply to every proposal?"
    a: "We read every single one, but we cannot reply to everybody. We receive far more proposals than we can programme and we are a small team. If you do not hear back, it does not mean your proposal was of no interest: it means it does not fit this year. We keep them all, and a proposal that does not fit now may fit a later edition."
  - q: "When and where does Festival ADAR take place?"
    a: "In August, in rural Asturias, northern Spain. Concerts happen in hórreos (traditional raised granaries), churches, palaces, monasteries and outdoors, in villages across the centre and west of the region. Admission is free."
  - q: "Is there a deadline for proposals?"
    a: "There is no fixed deadline: you can write at any time of year. Each edition's programme is settled during the preceding winter, so the earlier a proposal arrives, the better its chances for that edition."
  - q: "How much does Festival ADAR pay artists?"
    a: "ADAR is a small festival with a small budget. We cannot match the fees of a large festival, and we would rather say so before anyone wastes their time. That is why we ask for your fee in the first email: it tells us straight away whether we can have a conversation."

<!-- ── HOW TO SEND ──────────────────────────────────────────────────── -->
<section class="ed-section rc-top" id="send">
<div class="ed-wrap">

<div class="rc-send">
<p class="ed-stat-t">Write to</p>
<p class="rc-send-v"><a href="mailto:leiguardamusic@gmail.com?subject=PROPUESTA%20ADAR%20%C2%B7%20%5Bname%20of%20the%20group%20or%20soloist%5D%20%C2%B7%20%5Bline-up%5D">leiguardamusic@gmail.com</a></p>
<p class="ed-stat-t">Subject line, exactly like this</p>
<p class="rc-send-s">PROPUESTA ADAR · [name of the group or soloist] · [line-up]</p>
</div>

<div class="rc-callout">
<p><strong>The subject line really matters.</strong> Proposals land in the same inbox as everything else. If it starts with <code>PROPUESTA ADAR</code> it goes straight into its own folder and we read it properly; if it does not, it gets lost in the day's mail.</p>
<p>Keep it in capitals and in that order — and keep the Spanish words <code>PROPUESTA ADAR</code> even if you write to us in English. That way a single filter catches every proposal in every language. Two examples:</p>
<ul class="rc-when-list">
<li><code>PROPUESTA ADAR · Bretón Quartet · string quartet</code></li>
<li><code>PROPUESTA ADAR · Ana Ruiz &amp; Lía Montes · voice and theorbo</code></li>
</ul>
</div>

<p class="ed-prose rc-note">There is no form and there will not be one. An email, written by a person, reads better than a form.</p>

</div>
</section>

<!-- ── WHAT WE PROGRAMME ────────────────────────────────────────────── -->
<section class="ed-section" id="what-we-programme">
<div class="ed-wrap">
<div class="ed-head">
<p class="ed-eyebrow">What we programme</p>
<h2>Almost anything, except commercial music</h2>
<p class="ed-lede">ADAR is a festival of living arts in the countryside. We play in hórreos — the traditional raised granaries of Asturias — in churches, palaces, monasteries and in the open air, with free admission, in small villages. That shapes what fits far more than any genre label does.</p>
</div>

<div class="ed-twocol">

<article class="ed-card">
<h3>Yes, send it</h3>
<p>Classical and chamber music. Early music, on historical instruments or not. Jazz. Experimental and electronic music. Traditional music and music from other cultures. Contemporary composition and premieres.</p>
<p>Also projects that do not fit a single box: sound with visual art, with dance, with video, with the landscape. Sound walks. Installations. <strong>Site-specific work interests us particularly</strong>, because that is how we work.</p>
</article>

<article class="ed-card">
<h3>Not for now</h3>
<p>Rock, pop and commercial music generally. This is not a judgement on their quality: they do not fit what we programme or the places we play, which are a Romanesque church or the covered space under a granary, with no stage and usually no amplification.</p>
<p>If you are unsure whether your project belongs here, send it anyway and we will look.</p>
</article>

</div>
</div>
</section>

<!-- ── WHAT TO SEND ─────────────────────────────────────────────────── -->
<section class="ed-section" id="what-to-send">
<div class="ed-wrap">
<div class="ed-head">
<p class="ed-eyebrow">What to send</p>
<h2>Eight things, in one email</h2>
<p class="ed-lede">No forty-page dossiers and no heavy attachments. Links and short text. If any of this is missing we have to write and ask for it, and we may not get round to it.</p>
</div>

<ol class="rc-steps">

<li><div class="rc-step-body">
<h3>Who you are</h3>
<p>Name of the group or soloist, line-up, how many people would travel, city and country, and your website if you have one.</p>
</div></li>

<li><div class="rc-step-body">
<h3>Recordings</h3>
<p>This is the first thing we look at. Links to YouTube, Vimeo, Bandcamp, SoundCloud or your own site — <strong>please do not attach files</strong>, they get stuck in the mail.</p>
<p>We would rather have <strong>video of a live concert</strong>, one whole piece or movement, unedited, than a heavily produced studio demo. We want to hear how you sound in a room, not in a computer.</p>
</div></li>

<li><div class="rc-step-body">
<h3>The programme you propose</h3>
<p>The works, with composers and durations, and the total length. If you have more than one possible programme, send them: it helps us match you to a particular venue.</p>
</div></li>

<li><div class="rc-step-body">
<h3>A short biography</h3>
<p>Ten lines is plenty. What you have done, where you have played and what sets you apart. If there is a long dossier, link to it, but the email should make sense without opening it.</p>
</div></li>

<li><div class="rc-step-body">
<h3>Your fee</h3>
<p><strong>This is the thing most often left out and the thing we most need.</strong> Without a fee we cannot assess the proposal, and it forces us to write and ask.</p>
<p>Give us the all-in fee per concert, whether it includes VAT, and <strong>whether it covers travel, accommodation and per diems, and for how many people</strong>. If you have mobility funding from your country or an institution, say so: it changes the arithmetic considerably.</p>
</div></li>

<li><div class="rc-step-body">
<h3>Technical requirements</h3>
<p>Just the essentials: whether you need a piano or harpsichord and of what kind, amplification, power, projection, minimum space. Many of our venues are granaries and churches with no installation at all, so this decides whether a proposal is even possible.</p>
</div></li>

<li><div class="rc-step-body">
<h3>Availability</h3>
<p>The festival runs in <strong>August</strong>. Tell us which August dates would work and whether you have any flexibility; sometimes we can fit a concert in because you are already touring in the north of Spain.</p>
</div></li>

<li><div class="rc-step-body">
<h3>A direct contact</h3>
<p>Name, email and phone number of whoever decides. If an agency represents you, copy in the right person.</p>
</div></li>

</ol>
</div>
</section>

<section class="ed-section" id="what-happens-next">
<div class="ed-wrap">
<div class="ed-head">
<p class="ed-eyebrow">What happens next</p>
<h2>We read everything. We cannot answer everything.</h2>
</div>

<div class="rc-warning">
<p class="rc-warning-t">If you do not hear back, it is not a no to your work.</p>
<p>We receive far more proposals than we can programme, and we are a small team that also produces the festival. Answering each one individually would take weeks we do not have. We would rather say it plainly here than leave you waiting.</p>
</div>

<p class="ed-prose">What we do with all of them: <strong>read them, listen to them and keep them.</strong> When we put an edition together we go back through everything that has arrived during the year. A proposal that does not fit in 2027 may fit in 2028, or may suddenly fit because a new venue appears that suits it. That is why it is worth writing even when no open call is running.</p>

<p class="ed-prose">And one more thing, for honesty's sake: <strong>ADAR is a small festival with a small budget.</strong> We do not have the money to hire a lot of people or to pay large-festival fees. We say so up front so that nobody wastes their time. Even so, we love receiving proposals: it is how we come across people we would never otherwise have met.</p>

</div>
</section>

<!-- ── QUESTIONS ────────────────────────────────────────────────────── -->
<section class="ed-section" id="questions">
<div class="ed-wrap">
<div class="ed-head">
<p class="ed-eyebrow">Questions</p>
<h2>The ones we always get</h2>
</div>

<div class="ed-twocol">

<article class="ed-card">
<h3>Is there a deadline?</h3>
<p>No fixed deadline: write whenever you like. Each edition's programme is settled during the preceding winter, so the earlier it arrives, the better its chances for that edition.</p>
</article>

<article class="ed-card">
<h3>Can I write in English?</h3>
<p>Yes — in English, Spanish or Asturian. The only thing we ask is that the subject line goes exactly as it appears above, in capitals and starting with <code>PROPUESTA ADAR</code>, whatever language the message is in.</p>
</article>

<article class="ed-card">
<h3>Do I have to be from Asturias?</h3>
<p>No. We programme artists from anywhere. That said, travel comes out of the same budget as the fee, so if you are coming from far away it helps to tell us whether you have any mobility funding.</p>
</article>

<article class="ed-card">
<h3>What if I wrote last year?</h3>
<p>Write again, especially if you have new recordings or a different programme. A short email recalling the earlier proposal and saying what has changed is enough.</p>
</article>

<article class="ed-card">
<h3>Is this the same as the residency?</h3>
<p>No. The <a href="/en/artist-residency-open-call-2026-27/">artist residency</a> is a different thing: a three- to four-week stay to create, with its own call and deadline. This page is for proposing a concert.</p>
</article>

<article class="ed-card">
<h3>Do you programme premieres?</h3>
<p>Yes, and we are interested in them. If the proposal includes a new work, tell us whose it is, whether it is finished, and whether there is a commission or any funding behind it.</p>
</article>

</div>
