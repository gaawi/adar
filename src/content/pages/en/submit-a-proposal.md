  - q: "How do I send a proposal to Festival ADAR?"
    a: "By email to leiguardamusic@gmail.com, with «PROPUESTA ADAR · [your name or the group's] · [line-up]» in the subject line. There is no form. We prefer email to phone calls."
  - q: "What should I send in the proposal?"
    a: "Who you are and how many of you would come, a link so we can hear you (video of a live concert is better than a studio recording), what you want to play with durations, four lines of biography, your fee, what you need technically, which August dates you can do, and a phone number."
  - q: "Do I have to give a fee?"
    a: "Yes, always. It is the thing most often left out and without it we cannot assess anything. Tell us the all-in fee, whether it includes VAT, and whether it covers travel, beds and food, and for how many people. If you have mobility funding from your country or an institution, tell us that too."
  - q: "Which proposals stand the best chance of being programmed?"
    a: "The ones that do something more than the concert. The festival is funded largely by grants for the development of the arts in rural areas, so it is far easier for us to make the case for a project that includes residency days in the village, a programme thought out for the place where it will be played, something open with the people who live here, or a real connection with Asturias."
  - q: "Does Festival ADAR do exchanges with other festivals or venues?"
    a: "Yes, and it is the format that works best: a group comes to play at ADAR and one of our musicians goes to play at their venue, series or festival. That way the travel is shared between two programmes. With groups from Spain, from Europe and from outside Europe."
  - q: "I am a manager. Can I send my artist roster?"
    a: "Yes, but send it filtered: only the artists you genuinely see fitting this festival and who have August free. A list of twenty with no criteria gets skimmed and closed; three well chosen get looked at one by one. Tell us each one's fee and their real dates."
  - q: "Do you reply to every proposal?"
    a: "We read them all and we listen to them all, but we do not reply to them all. There are very few of us and the festival is produced by the same hands that read the email. No reply does not mean we did not like it: we keep them and look again when we put each edition together."
  - q: "When and where is Festival ADAR?"
    a: "In August, in villages across the centre and west of Asturias, in northern Spain. We play in hórreos — the traditional raised granaries — in churches, palaces, a monastery and in the open air. Admission is free."
  - q: "Is there a deadline for proposals?"
    a: "No deadline. Each edition's programme is settled in winter, so if it arrives before then we can look at it properly."

<h2 class="amp-title">If you want to play at <em>ADAR</em></h2>
<p class="amp-subtitle">Write to us. Here is what we programme, what we need to know, and why we sometimes take a while to reply.</p>
</div>
</div>

<section class="ed-section rc-top">
<div class="ed-wrap">

<p class="ed-prose">We get a lot of emails from musicians and agencies who want to play at the festival, and we are glad they arrive: a good part of the people who have passed through ADAR turned up that way, writing without anyone having called them. So yes, write to us.</p>

<p class="ed-prose">What follows is not a set of rules or a form. It is what helps us decide, told the way we would tell a colleague.</p>

<div class="rc-send">
<p class="ed-stat-t">Our email</p>
<p class="rc-send-v"><a href="mailto:leiguardamusic@gmail.com?subject=PROPUESTA%20ADAR%20%C2%B7%20%5Byour%20name%20or%20the%20group%27s%5D%20%C2%B7%20%5Bline-up%5D">leiguardamusic@gmail.com</a></p>
<p class="ed-stat-t">Put this in the subject line</p>
<p class="rc-send-s">PROPUESTA ADAR · [your name or the group's] · [line-up]</p>
</div>

<p class="ed-prose">We ask for that subject line because everything comes into the same inbox. If it starts with <code>PROPUESTA ADAR</code> we can gather them and read them in one sitting in winter, which is when the programme gets decided. If it does not, it gets lost in everything else. Keep the Spanish words even when you write to us in English — that way a single filter catches them all — in capitals, then your name and the line-up: <code>PROPUESTA ADAR · Bretón Quartet · string quartet</code>.</p>

<p class="ed-prose">We prefer email to phone calls. There is no form and there is not going to be one.</p>

</div>
</section>

<!-- ── WHAT GETS PLAYED HERE ────────────────────────────────────────── -->
<section class="ed-section">
<div class="ed-wrap">
<div class="ed-head">
<h2>What gets played here</h2>
</div>

<p class="ed-prose">ADAR is a small festival in villages in Asturias, in the north of Spain. We play in hórreos — the raised stone granaries you see all over the region — in Romanesque churches, in a monastery, in palaces and in the open air. There is almost never a stage and almost never any amplification: you play inside a stone building that has been standing for eight hundred years and the sound is what it is. That decides what fits far more than the genre label does.</p>

<p class="ed-prose">We programme classical and chamber music, early music — on historical instruments or not — jazz, experimental, electronic, traditional music and music from other cultures, and contemporary composition. Also things that are not exactly a concert: sound walks, installations, projects with video or with dance. If it is conceived for a particular place, better still, because that is how we work ourselves.</p>

<p class="ed-prose">What we do not programme is rock, pop and commercial music. It is not a judgement on anyone: it just does not work in the places we play. If you are not sure whether your thing belongs here, send it anyway and we will look.</p>

</div>
</section>

<!-- ── WHAT STANDS THE BEST CHANCE ──────────────────────────────────── -->
<section class="ed-section">
<div class="ed-wrap">
<div class="ed-head">
<h2>What stands the best chance</h2>
</div>

<p class="ed-prose">Let us save you some time with something that does not always get said. The festival is kept going largely by grants for the development of the arts in rural areas. That is not paperwork: it is what ADAR is. And it means that a concert on its own is much harder for us to make the case for than a project that also leaves something behind here.</p>

<p class="ed-prose">What stands the best chance, roughly in this order:</p>

<ul class="rc-when-list rc-managers">
<li><strong>Coming a few days earlier to work in the village.</strong> We have a <a href="/en/adar-artistic-residencies/">residency house</a> in Leiguarda. A group that arrives, stays, rehearses, tries things out and then plays is nothing like a group that arrives in the afternoon and leaves at night. You can hear it in the concert.</li>
<li><strong>A programme thought out for the place.</strong> It does not need reinventing: sometimes it is just choosing different repertoire knowing you will be playing in a twelfth-century church and not in a concert hall.</li>
<li><strong>Something with the people who live here.</strong> A workshop, an open rehearsal, an hour with the children at the school or with the older people in the village. It does not have to be complicated; it just has to be thought through rather than tacked on at the last minute.</li>
<li><strong>A connection with Asturias,</strong> with rural heritage or with the memory of the place — in the repertoire, in the material, or in who you collaborate with.</li>
</ul>

<p class="ed-prose">You do not need all four. One, properly thought out, is plenty.</p>

</div>
</section>

<!-- ── EXCHANGES ────────────────────────────────────────────────────── -->
<section class="ed-section">
<div class="ed-wrap">
<div class="ed-head">
<h2>If you programme too, let's talk about an exchange</h2>
</div>

<p class="ed-prose">This interests us a great deal and almost nobody proposes it. If as well as playing you run a venue, a concert series or a festival, tell us in your first email. The format that works best for us is an exchange: you come and play at ADAR, and one of our musicians goes to play at your place.</p>

<p class="ed-prose">That way the travel is shared between two programmes instead of falling entirely on one, and both of us open up a circuit. It makes no difference whether you are just down the road, anywhere in Europe or on the other side of the world. Tell us what space you have, when you programme and what kind of room it is, and let's talk.</p>

</div>
</section>

<!-- ── WHAT TO SEND US ──────────────────────────────────────────────── -->
<section class="ed-section">
<div class="ed-wrap">
<div class="ed-head">
<h2>What we need you to send us</h2>
<p class="ed-lede">Links and not much text. If something is missing we have to write and ask for it, and that is where the weeks go.</p>
</div>

<ol class="rc-steps">

<li><div class="rc-step-body">
<h3>Who you are and how many of you come</h3>
<p>The name, the line-up, how many people would travel, where you are from, and your website if you have one. How many of you come matters more than it sounds: out here, beds and a van count.</p>
</div></li>

<li><div class="rc-step-body">
<h3>Something we can listen to</h3>
<p>A link: YouTube, Vimeo, Bandcamp, SoundCloud, your own site. Please do not send attachments, they get stuck in the mail.</p>
<p>And if you have a choice, send us <strong>video of a concert</strong>: one whole piece or movement, no cuts, with an audience in front of you. It tells us far more than a carefully produced studio recording. We want to hear how you sound in a room.</p>
</div></li>

<li><div class="rc-step-body">
<h3>What you want to play</h3>
<p>The works, with composers and durations, and how long the whole thing runs. If you have several programmes, send them all: it helps us place you in one venue or another.</p>
</div></li>

<li><div class="rc-step-body">
<h3>Four lines about yourselves</h3>
<p>Four lines will do. If you have a long dossier, send the link, but the email should make sense without opening it.</p>
</div></li>

<li><div class="rc-step-body">
<h3>The fee</h3>
<p>This is the thing most often left out and the thing we most need. Without a fee we cannot assess anything, and it forces us to write and ask.</p>
<p>Tell us the all-in fee, whether it includes VAT, and whether it covers travel, beds and food, and for how many people. And if you have mobility funding from your country or an institution, say so: it changes the whole conversation.</p>
</div></li>

<li><div class="rc-step-body">
<h3>What you need technically</h3>
<p>Whether you need a piano or harpsichord and what kind, amplification, light, power, projection, how much space. A lot of our venues are granaries and churches with no installation of any kind, so this sometimes decides whether a proposal is possible at all.</p>
</div></li>

<li><div class="rc-step-body">
<h3>Which August dates you can do</h3>
<p>The festival is in August. Tell us which days you have free and whether there is any give. And if you are already touring the north of Spain around then, say so: sometimes that is exactly what makes the numbers work.</p>
</div></li>

<li><div class="rc-step-body">
<h3>A phone number</h3>
<p>We work by email and we prefer it that way. But leave us your number anyway: once a proposal is moving, whatever is left to settle gets settled in five minutes on the phone. We will call you.</p>
</div></li>

</ol>
</div>
</section>

<!-- ── MANAGERS ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────── -->
<section class="ed-section">
<div class="ed-wrap">
<div class="ed-head">
<h2>If you are a manager</h2>
</div>

<p class="ed-prose">Send us your whole roster, with the same details as above for each artist. But send it filtered, and we mean this: if a list of twenty artists arrives with no criteria behind it, we skim it and close it. If three arrive that you have chosen thinking about this festival, we look at them one by one and we reply.</p>

<p class="ed-prose">For each one we need the fee and the August dates they genuinely have free, not a generic availability. And if any of your artists work in residency, in unusual spaces or with communities, put that in the first line: those stand the best chance of getting in.</p>

<p class="ed-prose">One email per submission, with the same subject line. If you want to add someone later, reply on that same thread so it does not scatter.</p>

</div>
</section>

<!-- ── WHY WE SOMETIMES DO NOT REPLY ────────────────────────────────── -->
<section class="ed-section">
<div class="ed-wrap">
<div class="ed-head">
<h2>Why we sometimes do not reply</h2>
</div>

<p class="ed-prose">We read everything and we listen to everything. But we do not reply to everything, and we would rather say so here than leave you waiting for an answer that never comes. There are very few of us, and the festival is produced by the same hands that read the email; replying one by one would take weeks we do not have.</p>

<p class="ed-prose">No reply does not mean we did not like it. It means it did not fit that year. We keep every proposal and look at them again when we put each edition together: some that did not fit have come in two years later, or have come in suddenly because a new venue appeared that suited them. That is why it is worth writing even when there is no open call.</p>

<p class="ed-prose">And the other thing we owe you up front: this is a small festival with a small budget. We cannot pay what a large festival pays, and we would rather you knew before you waste your time. That is why we ask for the fee in the first email — that way we both know straight away whether there is room to talk.</p>

</div>
</section>

<section class="ed-section">
<div class="ed-wrap">
<div class="ed-head">
<h2>Other things people ask us</h2>
</div>

<div class="ed-twocol">

<article class="ed-card">
<h3>Is there a deadline?</h3>
<p>No. Each edition's programme is settled in winter, so if it arrives before then we can look at it properly. But write whenever you like.</p>
</article>

<article class="ed-card">
<h3>Which language should I write in?</h3>
<p>Spanish, English or Asturian, whichever suits you. All we ask is that the subject line stays exactly as it is, including when you write in English.</p>
</article>

<article class="ed-card">
<h3>Do I have to be from here?</h3>
<p>No. People come from everywhere. The one thing: travel comes out of the same money as the fee, so if you are coming from far away tell us whether you have any mobility funding.</p>
</article>

<article class="ed-card">
<h3>I wrote to you last year</h3>
<p>Write again, especially if you have new recordings or a different programme. A short email reminding us of the last one and what has changed is enough.</p>
</article>

<article class="ed-card">
<h3>Is this the residency?</h3>
<p>No, they are two different things. The <a href="/en/artist-residency-open-call-2026-27/">residency</a> has its own call and its own deadline. This page is for proposing a concert, even one with residency days inside it.</p>
</article>

<article class="ed-card">
<h3>Do you programme premieres?</h3>
<p>Yes, and they interest us. Tell us whose the work is, whether it is finished, and whether there is a commission or any funding behind it.</p>
</article>
