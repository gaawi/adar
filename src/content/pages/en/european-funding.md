<h2 class="amp-title">European <em>funding</em></h2>
<p class="amp-subtitle">ADAR's artistic residency in Leiguarda is funded by the European Union through Culture Moves Europe.</p>
</div>
</div>

<section class="ed-section rc-top">
<div class="ed-wrap">

<p class="ed-prose">The <a href="/en/adar-artistic-residencies/">artistic residency</a> that ADAR hosts in Leiguarda is <strong>funded by the European Union</strong> through <strong>Culture Moves Europe</strong>, the mobility programme of Creative Europe, and <strong>implemented by the Goethe-Institut</strong>.</p>

<p class="ed-prose">The Asociación para el Desarrollo de las Artes en el Medio Rural was selected as a hosting organisation in the <em>Call for Residency Hosts 2025–2026</em>. The grant covers accommodation and mentoring for the resident artists, together with their daily allowance and travel costs.</p>

<p class="ed-prose">The project is a twenty-one-day stay in Leiguarda, in the municipality of Belmonte de Miranda, for a group of five artists and cultural professionals living in other European countries. The implementation period runs until 7 June 2027.</p>
