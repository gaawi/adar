<p class="ed-eyebrow">What it is</p>
<h2>Hórreos and paneras as a stage</h2>
<p class="ed-lede">Every year ADAR opens a call to choose hórreos and paneras across Asturias to host concerts produced by the festival, with free entry. Each selected space receives a prize (€300 per hórreo) and features in the festival's communication —website, posters, press and video. A curatorial jury weighs heritage value, conditions for chamber music, the setting and the ties to the local community.</p>

<details class="hc-bases">
<summary>2026 rules · 6th edition</summary>
<div class="hc-bases-body">

<p class="hc-bases-meta">Published on 16 June 2026 · call open until 25 July 2026. Contact: <a href="mailto:leiguardamusic@gmail.com">leiguardamusic@gmail.com</a> · <a href="https://wa.me/34663604030">+34 663 604 030</a>.</p>

<h4>1. Purpose</h4>
<p>To select two hórreos or paneras in Asturias to host two concerts produced by ADAR during August 2026, as part of the festival's sixth edition.</p>

<h4>2. Who can apply</h4>
<p>Owners of the hórreo or panera; town councils; legally constituted neighbourhood and cultural associations; or anyone with a proven link to the space, with the owner's written authorisation.</p>

<h4>3. Requirements of the space</h4>
<p>Located in Asturias; a hórreo or panera of heritage value; in adequate condition; with road access sufficient for ADAR's light technical equipment; and a well-kept setting.</p>

<h4>4. Timeline</h4>
<p>Applications until 23:59 on 25 July 2026. Winners announced 1 August 2026. Concerts: 4 August (harpsichord recital by Ignacio Prego) and 14 August (residency showing by the trio The Rest Project).</p>

<h4>5. Documentation</h4>
<p>In a single email to <a href="mailto:leiguardamusic@gmail.com">leiguardamusic@gmail.com</a> (subject "Candidatura Concurso Hórreos 2026"): applicant details; exact location (address, concejo and GPS if possible); three photographs (general view, construction detail and surroundings); a short description (max. 300 words); and the owner's authorisation where applicable.</p>

<h4>6. Jury and criteria</h4>
<p>A curatorial jury appointed by ADAR assesses: heritage value and architectural singularity; acoustic and staging conditions for chamber music; setting and accessibility; ties to the community; and territorial diversity (the two venues should be in different concejos). The decision is final.</p>

<h4>7. Prize</h4>
<p>€300 per hórreo, towards the cost of lending the space. Each venue hosts a concert produced entirely by ADAR, free of charge, and features in the festival's official communication.</p>

<h4>8. Obligations of the selected spaces</h4>
<p>Provide access on the agreed dates; accept the minimal technical set-up (lighting, sound, chairs); allow photographic and video documentation and its later distribution; and help with local outreach.</p>

<p class="hc-bases-foot">Data protection under GDPR (EU) 2016/679 and Spanish Law 3/2018; rights of access, rectification and erasure at leiguardamusic@gmail.com. Submitting an application implies full acceptance of these rules.</p>
