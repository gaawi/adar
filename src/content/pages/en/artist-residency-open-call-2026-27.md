<!-- ── THE RESIDENCY ────────────────────────────────────────────────── -->
<section class="ed-section">
<div class="ed-wrap">
<div class="ed-head">
<p class="ed-eyebrow">The residency</p>
<h2>Time to work, in a village</h2>
<p class="ed-lede">ADAR invites groups of 4–5 artists and cultural professionals to spend 21 to 30 consecutive days developing a project in Leiguarda, a small rural village in Asturias, northern Spain.</p>
</div>

<div class="ed-twocol">
<article class="ed-card">
<h3>A space for</h3>
<p>Research. Creation. Experimentation. Rehearsal. Artistic development. Preparation of a future work, recording, exhibition or performance.</p>
<p><strong>A finished work is not required.</strong> The proposal can be simple, and it can be one stage of a larger project you will complete later.</p>
</article>

<article class="ed-card">
<h3>What ADAR provides</h3>
<p>Accommodation for the whole stay. Working and rehearsal space. Mentoring, and organisational and artistic support throughout the residency.</p>
<p>ADAR's hosting costs are covered separately and are not part of the money paid to residents.</p>
</article>
</div>

</div>
</section>

<!-- ── GROUP + ELIGIBILITY ──────────────────────────────────────────── -->
<section class="ed-section">
<div class="ed-wrap">
<div class="ed-head">
<p class="ed-eyebrow">Who can apply</p>
<h2>Groups of 4–5, one single application</h2>
</div>

<div class="ed-twocol">
<article class="ed-card">
<h3>Apply as one group</h3>
<p>Only <strong>one person</strong> sends the application, on behalf of everyone. The group may already work together, or may come together specifically for this residency.</p>
</article>

<article class="ed-card">
<h3>Each member must</h3>
<p>Be at least 18 years old. Be an artist or cultural professional in an eligible Culture Moves Europe sector. Legally reside in a Creative Europe country <strong>other than Spain</strong>.</p>
</article>
</div>

<div class="rc-warning">
<p class="rc-warning-t">You cannot apply if you legally reside in Spain.</p>
<p>Nationality does not determine eligibility — legal residence does. A Spanish artist legally residing in France, for example, may be eligible.</p>
</div>

<p class="ed-prose rc-note"><strong>Eligible sectors:</strong> architecture · cultural heritage · design and fashion design · literature · music · performing arts · visual arts.</p>

</div>
</section>

<section class="ed-section" id="faq">
<div class="ed-wrap">
<div class="ed-head">
<p class="ed-eyebrow">FAQ</p>
<h2>Quick answers</h2>
</div>

<div class="ed-twocol">
<article class="ed-card"><h3>Can I apply alone?</h3><p>No. This call is for groups of 4–5 residents.</p></article>
<article class="ed-card"><h3>Does everyone have to live in the same country?</h3><p>No. Members may legally reside in different eligible Creative Europe countries — but none of them in Spain.</p></article>
<article class="ed-card"><h3>Does nationality matter?</h3><p>No. Only legal residence counts.</p></article>
<article class="ed-card"><h3>Do we need to be an established group?</h3><p>No. You can form a group specifically for this residency.</p></article>
<article class="ed-card"><h3>Do we need a full project proposal?</h3><p>No. A few sentences are enough for the initial application.</p></article>
<article class="ed-card"><h3>Do we need to send CVs?</h3><p>Not at this stage.</p></article>
<article class="ed-card"><h3>Do we have to produce a finished work?</h3><p>No. Research, experimentation, rehearsal, creation or developing a future project are all valid.</p></article>
<article class="ed-card"><h3>How long is the residency?</h3><p>Between 21 and 30 consecutive days, completed before 7 June 2027.</p></article>
<article class="ed-card"><h3>Is accommodation included?</h3><p>Yes, provided by ADAR on top of the financial support.</p></article>
<article class="ed-card"><h3>Can we prepare a future album, performance or exhibition?</h3><p>Yes.</p></article>
